# How the Liftory screens work

How the screens work, with the names the app shows:

Libraries (Exercises, Techniques, Foods)
- Search, the library chips and the star (favourites) narrow the list; the + button adds the user's own item into the library they choose, and a new library name creates one.
- Holding one of their own libraries (right-click on a computer), or its ⋯, offers Rename Library and Delete.
- Tapping an exercise or a technique opens its options: Edit and Delete; Reset to default on one built on a shipped item; Reset to trainer version on a copy a coach shared, which carries a small link icon. Techniques also have Move to library.
- An exercise a plan uses cannot be deleted; the message names the plan, and the exercise has to come out of that plan first. A deleted shipped exercise or technique comes back from the refresh icon in the toolbar.
- A library named after an email address holds what that coach sent with a plan: copies of the exercises and techniques, and the coach's foods under Shared in Foods. An exercise, technique or food copy is the user's to edit (a food's Reset to trainer version is inside its edit sheet); Reset to trainer version puts the coach's current version back, and the coach's next save of a plan that uses it does too. It needs a connection and works only while that coach coaches them. The copy shows the coach's picture until the user uploads their own, which is then kept.

Training plans (Create Training)
- Two tabs: My Plans (a plan has a date and a state, Active or Scheduled) and Templates (searchable, grouped by library).
- The + on My Plans opens Quick start: how many weeks, which days, what each day is for, Every new card starts at, Exercises per day and Progression. Build the plan writes it and opens the editor; Start from a template goes to Templates. The + on Templates asks for a name and a library.
- Tapping a plan offers Edit, Save as template, Export as PDF and Delete; a template offers Edit, Duplicate, Change library, Use template to create plan (asks only for the start date), Export as PDF and Delete. Save as template and Duplicate keep the template at once, with a toast naming it. Export as PDF is a Pro feature.
- In the editor: Week 1 in the status strip switches weeks and holds Add blank week, Clone current week and Delete week; the + on the Day rail adds a day and its ... renames or deletes it. Add exercise and Add block (Superset, Circuit, Drop set, Timed finisher) sit at the foot of the day. Tapping a row opens its card: Sets, Reps, Rest, the weeks strip and Tools (a preview, written by Apply). The card's shape row sets the technique: Set card, Duration card, Drop set, Link with the next, and the others under Written into the plan and the PDF. Why this plan holds the plan's reasons, never anything about health. Save writes the plan and shows how many things still need fixing; deletes leave an Undo instead of asking; leaving unsaved work asks (Stay, Leave).
- A coach assigns a plan by writing it from the client's page; Activate for client makes a plan the one the client follows.

Nutrition plans (Write a nutrition plan)
- Three tabs: Menus (the one being followed first, marked Following, then the earlier ones), Templates and Meals. On a client's list, Menus is Their menus; Templates and Meals are always the coach's own.
- The plus on Menus asks Start from this plan, Start from a template or Start from an empty plan. Tapping a menu offers Open, Follow this again (an earlier menu), Keep as template, Export as PDF and Delete (only an earlier menu with nothing logged under it).
- In the editor, Add a meal offers names with their hours (Breakfast 08:00, Lunch 14:00 and so on) or Another name. Inside a meal, Add a food opens the food list: tap every food the version needs, then Done; three letters also search Packaged products; Add it to your foods adds a missing one. An empty version also offers Add from my meals.
- Another version asks which day it is for (on a plan with more than one kind of day), then Blank or From my meals. From my meals opens Which meal?: a search by meal name or by a food in it, the meals grouped by library (their own libraries, My meals, Liftory starters), and From my templates.
- A line's ⋯ holds Optional, Recommended, Note about this food, Offer an alternative (or), Rename this line, Amount in words, Add a food to this line, Select lines, Move up, Move down and Delete. A version's ⋯ holds Duplicate, Duplicate for another kind of day, Move up, Move down, Save to my meals and Delete.
- Write the meals for me, in the plan header, writes the meals from the plan's targets and week. Why this menu, under the notes at the foot, holds the menu's reasons; foods left out are preferences, never allergies, and nothing about health goes there.
- At the foot: Save plan makes it the one the client follows and retires the previous one; Keep as template and Save to my meals keep their copy at once; Export as PDF is a Pro feature. Deleting a meal, a version or a line happens at once, with Undo on the line at the foot of the screen.
- Templates tab: tapping a template offers Edit, Start a menu from it (for the client when the list is open on one), Start a menu for a client, Duplicate, Change library, Export as PDF and Delete.
- Meals tab: every coach starts with 24 meals in Liftory starters, which are ordinary meals to edit or delete. The plus asks for a name and opens the meal editor, where Save keeps it. Tapping a meal offers Edit, Duplicate, Move to library and Delete (at once, with Undo); the ⋯ on a library's chip renames or deletes the library and keeps its meals.
- A coach's meal used in a client's saved plan is copied to the client, in a library named after the coach. On their Meals tab the client can Edit, Duplicate, Move to library or Delete it, or put it back with Reset to trainer version; the coach's next save of that plan brings the copy up to date.

Logging the nutrition day (Nutrition)
- The meal that is due is already open: As planned logs it, Skipped records that it was not eaten, Something else records what was eaten instead. Other versions each have their own Log.
- After logging, the meal stays open for Servings, which food of a choice, the amounts, Edit amounts (Didn't have it), Change meal, Remove from my log and How to make it; every change offers Undo.
- Something else, on a meal or under the meals: From my foods picks a food and asks how much; From my coach's meals (only for someone holding a coach's meals) opens My coach's meals and logs a whole meal in one tap, with Undo. It counts towards what they ate, never as following the plan.
- A day ahead can be read, not logged; a past day can still be logged.
- Someone with no plan sees Write my own. A client can change a plan their coach wrote; the notice at the top offers Message your coach.

Being coached (clients)
- The coach card (Find Trainer) holds Program, the sessions the coach owes them with Join for an online one, Messages, Book (with the sessions left), What your coach can see, the paid sessions left with See record, and at its foot Manage payment or End coaching; a request not yet accepted reads Pending Approval, with × to withdraw it.
- Book a session: Your booked sessions lists the hours already held, and tapping one offers Add to Calendar, Join Meeting, Decline, or Cancel free session for a free hour. A new booking asks What for and Pay with, or Or buy a package; the calendar's legend reads Available, Free, Yours, Taken and Outside working hours. A client on a monthly plan with no sessions left reads Your coach sets the schedule, with Message your coach.
- Purchases: Cancel billing (it asks Stop payments?), Resume billing, Change plan, Manage and Contact your trainer per coach; From Liftory for Pro and coins, with Buy Pro or Muscle Coins. A refund is agreed with the coach, who settles it.
- Training: the plan's row reads Continue · Week N, Day N (Start plan before the first day), with week chips and Download plan images. A day reads Next, In progress (Resume), Done or Rest day; the week's toolbar holds Edit (the pencil) and Reset progress (the circular arrow, which asks Reset Progress? and keeps the logged workouts), and opening a day already done asks Restart Day?.
- Workout: Start Workout. The list view shows Today's Progress and End; the card view logs with Complete Set N, then runs the rest timer, and shows Average and Previous. Add Exercise adds one, Edit Set N corrects a logged set, End Workout Early? asks before ending with sets left, and Workout Complete! offers Share, View Stats and Done. Starting another day while one runs asks Switch workout?.
- Stats: Overview, Progress and Records, Pick a date for a day, Session metrics on a workout recorded with a watch, and a workout's ⋮ for Share and Delete workout.
- Body checks: one a day, added with +, changed with Edit Body Check; the camera shows an outline and a timer; Trends and Compare read them, and Export PDF is a Pro feature. Body checks are the user's own log: never ask for one or for what it contains.

Paying a coach (clients)
- Joining with an invite code: Find Trainer, the key button in the corner (I have an invite code), then the code in Join trainer and Join. It charges nothing; Purchases lists it under Coaching you pay for directly, with the date it ends.
- Cancel billing (Stop payments?) keeps the coaching until the paid period ends; the row then reads Cancels soon with the date, and Resume billing takes it back. Change plan appears only where the coach allows a move. One subscription per coach; single sessions and packs can always be bought, and someone still holding sessions is told so and asked Buy anyway.

Coaching (coaches)
- Dashboard: the Clients and Business tabs. Clients has a search and the chips All, Needs you, Unpaid, Active, New and Lapsed; each Needs you card offers its fixes: Accept or Reject, Mark paid or Remind, Message, Snooze, Open client. A client who cancelled their card billing reads Cancels Soon with the end date (Ends …) on their row and at the top of their page until the period closes.
- One client: Message, Note and the ⋯ (Edit Price, Extend subscription, End subscription) at the top; the tiles Training, Nutrition, Sessions, Stats, Body and Extend; then the sections Progress, Training plan, Nutrition, Money, Notes and Activity. Mark paid records a payment, Log a session and Adjust change the sessions left, and Archive files away a client whose coaching has ended. Notes and follow-ups are never shown to the client. The demo client goes with End subscription.
- Schedule: the clock (Bookable hours), the eye (Preview as client) and the plus (New appointment). Dragging across the calendar opens New appointment on that range; on a phone, press and hold first. The form asks Who is it for, the title (PT Session to start), Starts and Ends, Duration, In person or Online (Online makes the video room; Meeting link takes their own room) and Add notes; Save is in the top bar. The client is not asked and can Decline.
- Tapping a booking shows This happened (once its start has passed), Edit appointment, Add to Calendar and Cancel Appointment, and Join for an online session; the client's picture opens their page. Booking an hour spends one of the client's sessions, except for a Free service.
- Saving a new hour for a client with no sessions left asks first (No sessions left, Book anyway or Cancel); a client who pays by the month is never asked, the fee covers their hours.
- Bookable hours (titled When you can be booked): Day, From, To, Slot and Services, then Add these hours. Services left empty reads Any paid service; a Free service sells only in a window that names it. Block a date, then Block this date, adds time off.
- Professional profile: Professional type; Discovery and pricing (Appear in trainer discovery, Accept requests from anyone, Show price on profile); the specialties and languages; Social and website; Location (Service Area, Delivery mode, Map visibility, Availability, the gym); Experience and certifications; Services and programs; Portfolio (up to six photos, each cropped to a portrait, and three videos, which can carry a caption); and Preview your public profile at the foot. Changes are saved as they are made, and a field left empty is emptied on Liftory too.
- A service, under Services and programs (Add service, or tap one): Price is Not for sale (what a new service starts as, not bookable), Free (bookable at no cost, always Per session) or Priced (with the amount). Billing period is Per session, Per month or Per plan; a Per plan service needs Sessions included. Then Session length and Where it happens (Same as my profile, In person, Online, In person & online).
- Business tab: the Coach Agreement banner until it is accepted; This month (Paid in Liftory, You collected it, Still waiting with Chase, Recurring, Still to deliver); the payments with the All, In Liftory and Outside filters and Mark paid on an unpaid row; How you get paid (Card payments in Liftory with Finish setup or Open Stripe, and You collect it yourself with New code); Invite codes; Get discovered (the QR code, Share profile, Download QR).
- Money is shown per currency and never added across currencies: a coach paid in francs and euros reads e.g. Still waiting CHF 80 · €120, and the split bar is left out while two currencies are in play.
- Invite codes: Business tab, then New code (Generate invite code): Subscription duration (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 12 months, Perpetual, Custom weeks), Single use, Code prefix, and Amount you collect yourself, which charges nobody; Generate shows the code as a link to send. Codes are listed under Invite codes (Active, Archived); the dots offer Copy, Share, Archive or Unarchive, and Delete.
- Money a coach collects themselves: on the client, Mark paid asks How did they pay? (Cash, Bank, TWINT, Other), then the amount, filled in with the agreed price. The row reads you marked it and has Edit (Save a new amount, or Delete); a card payment reads confirmed by Stripe and has no Edit. Remind opens the client's messages; nothing is chased automatically.
- Sessions on the client: Adjust (sessions to add, or a negative number to remove, and Why?) and Log a session (Which hour was it?, or An hour that is not in the calendar). The record reads Bought, Used, Returned, Adjusted and Refunded. Extend lengthens an invite-code arrangement (1, 3 or 6 months, a date, or no end).
- Selling by card needs Stripe set up (Finish setup) and the Coach Agreement accepted; a Free service needs neither. Picking an hour to pay for holds it for five minutes (Awaiting Payment). Refunds are made from the coach's own Stripe dashboard; the app has no refund button. A full refund takes the sessions back; a partial one is settled with Adjust.
- Messages: one thread per person. Holding a conversation in the list deletes it; holding one of their own messages offers Edit, Resend and Copy, and both Edit and Resend send a new message, since a sent one cannot be changed.

Settings (everyone)
- The card at the top opens Edit profile. The Account list holds Connected assistants (each assistant allowed to use Liftory as them, with Disconnect), resending the verification email while the address is unverified, and Set a password for an account that was created by signing in with another service and so has none.
- Preferences: Language (English, German, French, Italian, Spanish), Theme (Auto (System), Light, Dark), Weight units (Kilograms or Pounds) and Measurement units (Centimeters or Inches); units change only what is shown.
- Automatic sync is on for everyone for now and cannot be switched off.
- Notifications: Messages on your phone, Messages by email, Sessions on your phone, Sessions by email and Plans on your phone, held for every device; on a phone, On this device (Notifications, Vibration, Sound, Importance) and the rest timer's sound.
- Ads: Remove ads without Pro; with Pro, Premium active with its date, Manage Subscription for a store or card purchase, and Purchases.
- Privacy: Share analytics, Export my data (everything as JSON), Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Open data and credits, and for a coach the Coach Agreement. A coach also has What you coach (Training, Nutrition) and Meals slipping (Days on the plan first, Meals logged below).
- Support: Send feedback, Rate the app, Restart tutorials. Delete account sits at the foot: it asks once, erases the person and keeps purchase records anonymised; a paid coach with clients still subscribed or holding sessions is shown those clients instead.
